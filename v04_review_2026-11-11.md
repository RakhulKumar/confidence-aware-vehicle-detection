# v0.4 results review (11 Nov 2026)

Source: `handoff_v04.zip` (fixes to v0.3 plus calibrator ablation; same cached predictions, no retraining). Class-agnostic matching, BMD-45 test role (1,541 images, 16,093 vehicles), 95% image-level bootstrap intervals.

## What changed from v0.3

- **Probe fixed.** Miss rate now rises monotonically with constructed occlusion (YOLO26, class-agnostic): 0.00, 0.13, 0.20, 0.29, 0.44, 0.71, 0.81, 0.83, 0.89, 0.94 at severity 0 to 0.9; native mode the same shape. Within-vehicle slope of uncertainty on severity 0.95 [0.88, 1.01] (was 0.52). This is now the cleanest causal evidence for RQ1.
- **Occlusion effect is not a size artefact.** Small vehicles are missed far more (YOLO26: 19.8% small, 11.9% medium, 7.6% large), but within every size tertile the miss rate still rises with occlusion (e.g. small 16.9% to ~40-45%, large 6.5% to 33%).
- **OCE fixed** (class-agnostic now ignores class). RT-DETR still best on OCE.
- **Ranking (RQ3b).** D-ECE separates no pair of detectors, at own thresholds or at matched cost. At matched cost (1.40 false alarms/image) MA-Brier says both YOLO models beat RT-DETR, OCE says RT-DETR beats both; neither separates YOLO26 from YOLOv8. Under the strict rule (both metrics must separate the pair, opposite signs) there is no "flip" in class-agnostic mode, because D-ECE separates nothing. Likely explanation, to be verified by decomposing MA-Brier: OCE ignores false alarms, MA-Brier counts them, and RT-DETR's false alarms are more confident.
- **Transfer (RQ4).** On AU-INV-PALS the 95% target is not deployable (38.6 flags/image YOLO26, 247 RT-DETR). At 90%: recalibrated coverage 0.902 at 3.6 flags/image, certifiable. Quote 90% for transfer.

## Calibrator ablation (selection on calib by 5-fold CV, before test)

Calib chose `full_strict` (duplicate feature restricted to IoU >= 0.5, disjoint from occlusion context) for all three detectors; test agrees. Mean recovery difference vs plain confidence on test:

| detector | all misses | occluded misses |
|---|---|---|
| YOLO26 | +0.045 [0.039, 0.051] | +0.019 [0.012, 0.027] |
| YOLOv8 | +0.032 [0.025, 0.040] | +0.002 [-0.005, 0.011] |
| RT-DETR | +0.060 [0.053, 0.067] | +0.062 [0.051, 0.072] |

- Most of the gain comes from down-ranking duplicate boxes (`z_dup` alone: +0.039 YOLO26).
- Occlusion context alone (`z_ctx`) gives nothing overall and hurts occluded misses for the YOLO models (-0.025, -0.029); it helps RT-DETR (+0.013).
- CV margins between `full`, `z_dup` and `full_strict` are within fold-to-fold noise (e.g. 0.217 vs 0.221, fold SD 0.018); the robust conclusions are "duplicates matter" and "context alone does not help YOLO".
- The learned score should be described as a duplicate- and context-aware score, not as the occlusion method. The occlusion-targeted method is the occlusion-conditional conformal rule (RQ2: +11.5 points on occluded misses at matched cost, YOLO26).

## Status of the research questions after v0.4

| RQ | Status |
|---|---|
| RQ1 | Supported: observational (odds x1.19-1.22 per +0.1, size and class controlled; holds within size tertiles) and causal probe (miss 0 to 0.94). |
| RQ2 | Supported in the form "certified recall (95% at ~4.5 flags/image; 90% at ~1) and occlusion-conditional coverage that recovers ~2x the occluded misses at equal cost, at the expense of unoccluded ones". Learned score adds +3-6 points overall, mostly via duplicates. |
| RQ3a | Supported (D-ECE 0.050 vs MA-ECE 0.159; D-ECE prefers a threshold with 4% recall; occluded vehicles over-represented 1.5x in its blind spot). |
| RQ3b | D-ECE cannot rank the detectors at all; miss-aware metrics can, but disagree with each other depending on whether false alarms count. Report as such. |
| RQ4 | Frozen guarantee breaks on transfer; re-fitting thresholds on target labels restores 90% at modest cost. PALS too sparse and incompletely labelled for occlusion transfer. |
| RQ5 | Supported (post-hoc proxy). |
